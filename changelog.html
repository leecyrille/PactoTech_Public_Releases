The Test screen now shows the raw electrical state of every input pin - including the mode pins - straight off the connectors, perfect for checking cabinet wiring (needs this firmware). The keyboard test shows just the keys being sent right now instead of a wall of every mapped key, and both keyboard keys and mode-pin presses are announced by voice.

The Help section now says which boards each feature applies to (a 2000T has no 4-player mode, twin stick isn't available on the 3000T, the 4000H uses button combos instead of TURBO/8TO6 pins, and so on).

When your board already updates from the app, the ST-Link flashing tool is dimmed with a note pointing at the normal update button - it stays available for recovery.

Corrects the ST-Link flashing guidance: when the chip can't be reached, the Utility now points at the usual causes - SWD wiring and making sure the board runs from its own power (an ST-Link's 3V3 pin can't power a whole board) - while it keeps retrying through a power-cycle.

20260707_0554 earlytesting App 2026-07-07

Fixes from early testing feedback.

Flashing with ST-Link is more resilient: if the chip can't be reached, the Utility keeps retrying with clear guidance - check the wiring, make sure the board has its own power, and power-cycle it. Detect once, then flash - no extra tools needed.

Test screen voice announcements now stop when the Utility window is in the background or closed to the tray.

Update checks and downloads no longer hang - a failed firmware download says so clearly and can be retried. The What's-new list now explains when releases exist on a different update channel than yours, and older installs are moved off the retired 'production' channel name automatically.

20260707_0419 earlytesting App 2026-07-06

The Utility can now flash boards with an ST-Link, right from the app.

If your board is on firmware from before in-app updates existed (or has the F103VB chip), you no longer need any external flashing tools: connect an ST-Link V2 to the board's SWD header, pick your board model in the Utility's firmware section, and press Flash. The app reads the chip itself, downloads the matching signed image, and refuses anything that doesn't fit your exact hardware.

Also fixes update-signature verification for in-app firmware updates.

20260707_0353 earlytesting App + Firmware 1000T-F103VC · 2000H-F103VB · 2000H-F103VC · 2000T-F103VC · 3000T-F103VC · 4000H-F103VB · 4000H-F103VC · 4000H2-F103ZE · 4000T-F103VC · SNAP2-F103VC · SNAP4-F103VC 2026-07-06

First Early Testing release of the new Pacto Tech software platform.

The Pacto Tech Utility for Windows: live controller status and input testing, full remapping for XInput and keyboard outputs, mode controls with editable shortcuts, customizable on-screen overlays and themes, kiosk/lockdown modes for cabinets, ready-made scripts for LaunchBox and other frontends, and automatic app updates on your chosen channel.

New firmware for every current board (1000T, 2000T, 2000H, 3000T, 4000T, 4000H, 4000H2, SNAP2, SNAP4 - including the F103VB chip variants of the 2000H/4000H and the F103ZE-based 4000H2) introduces in-app firmware updates: flash your board once with the matching ST-Link image from this release, and every update after that installs from inside the Utility with a couple of clicks. Updates are checked against your exact board model AND its chip, an interrupted update can't brick the board, and a file for the wrong board simply won't apply. Boards with the smaller F103VB chip keep updating via ST-Link (no room for the update system on that chip) - the Utility tells you when a new version is out for them.

Also improved: much cleaner switching between arcade (XInput) and gamepad/keyboard (DISS.) modes, per-input mode settings, twin-stick 1P/2P modes, and turbo refinements.
