Pacto Tech — Release Notes

Updates for the Pacto Tech Utility app and controller firmware.

20260707_0103 earlytesting App + Firmware 1000T · 2000H · 2000T · 4000H · 4000T 2026-07-06

First Early Testing release of the new Pacto Tech software platform.

The Pacto Tech Utility for Windows: live controller status and input testing, full remapping for XInput and keyboard outputs, mode controls with editable shortcuts, customizable on-screen overlays and themes, kiosk/lockdown modes for cabinets, ready-made scripts for LaunchBox and other frontends, and automatic app updates on your chosen channel.

New firmware for all current boards (1000T, 2000T, 2000H, 4000T, 4000H) introduces in-app firmware updates: flash your board once with the ST-Link image from this release, and every update after that installs from inside the Utility with a couple of clicks. An interrupted update can't brick the board. Also improved: much cleaner switching between arcade (XInput) and gamepad/keyboard (DISS.) modes, per-input mode settings, twin-stick 1P/2P modes, and turbo refinements.
